# Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of information held by the bot operator, email [octaviansasha@gmail.com](mailto:octaviansasha@gmail.com). Include the WhatsApp number used to contact the bot and describe the relevant task or message. The operator can remove local queued and pending bot data. Tasks and photo attachments already created in Asana must be deleted in Asana. WhatsApp chat history must be deleted in WhatsApp. Meta and Asana may retain records under their own retention policies.
